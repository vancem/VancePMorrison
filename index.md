# Vance Morrison

VancePMorrison

My name is Vance Morrison.   I was born in 1964 and have lived in Kirkland, Washington 
since 1996 with my wife Sharon.  

## Career
I am a software engineer and worked for over two decades at Microsoft before retiring in 2019.
While at Microsoft  I worked in the Developer Division on the .NET Runtime, mostly on 
the Just in Time compiler for the .NET Runtime.   Frequently I worked closely with the C# 
language team.   Later in my career I became a Performance Architects and wrote the 
[PerfView](https://github.com/microsoft/perfview) tool open source and still in use today.  

## Square Dance

My wife and I learned to square dance when we were in college at the University of Illinois at
Urbana-Champaign.  Like many people our dancing lapsed when we had kids, but we got back into
it in the 2000s.    I became a square dance caller in 2017.  Some of the documents / software 
below reflects my interest in square dancing.   I do believe it is one of the best general human activities:  Fun for all ages, easy to learn, cooperative, strongly social, and great exercise.
Do yourself a favor and search for 'Square Dance near me' in a web search to find a local club 
where you can get in on the fun.   If you live in the Seattle area, you are likely to see me
on the dance floor.  

## Published

Here are some of the documents/software I have published over the years.   

- [CallerTips](https://vancem.github.io/CallerTips/) ([GitHub](https://github.com/vancem/CallerTips))

    A free book for new square dance callers.

- [CallerBuddy](https://vancem.github.io/CallerBuddy/) ([GitHub](https://github.com/vancem/CallerBuddy))

    A music player and lyric manager for square dance callers.

- [Marble Tree](https://vancem.github.io/MarbleTree/) ([GitHub](https://github.com/vancem/MarbleTree))

    How to laser-cut and assemble a wooden marble tree.  If you have access to a laser
    cutter and have a 1-5 year old in your life, this is a must-build.  

- [PerfView GitHub](https://github.com/microsoft/perfview) 

    A tool for doing performance analysis on the Windows operating system.
